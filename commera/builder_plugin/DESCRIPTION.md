Design your Commera store themes in Builder.

On a page of a Commera theme, the toolbar shows the theme and the page you are on. Open it to:

- Switch between the theme's Home, Listing and Product pages, and see which are published, edited, draft or still empty.
- Pick the product or collection that the canvas previews.
- Publish every designed page of the theme at once. An empty page stays unpublished, so the store keeps the parent theme's page there.
- Activate the theme, so shoppers see it, and open the page on your store.

Create a theme in the Commera dashboard, at **Storefront > Themes > New theme**.
