Design your Commera store themes in Builder.

**Store themes**, in the dashboard sidebar, lists your themes: the live one first, then the theme library. From there you can:

- Make a new theme. It starts with empty Home, Listing and Product pages, and its Home page opens with Bob, so you can describe the design.
- Open a theme to see its three pages, and whether each is published, edited, a draft or still empty. A click opens the page in the editor.
- Publish every designed page of a theme at once. An empty page stays unpublished, so the store keeps the parent theme's page there.
- Switch the live theme, and open the live theme on your store.

On a page of a Commera theme, the toolbar shows the theme and the page you are on. Open it to switch pages, pick the product or collection that the canvas previews, publish the theme and activate it.

If Store themes and the theme bar are gone, check that plugins are on in **Settings > Plugins**.
